Centara Karon Resort Phuket

Discover beachside relaxation and family-friendly fun at Centara Karon Resort Phuket, where modern style meets the stunning beauty of the Andaman Sea. Our newly renovated resort harmoniously blends sleek contemporary design, vibrant hues, and lush natural surroundings to create a serene atmosphere with a variety of entertainment options for all ages. 

Simply unwind at our refreshing pool, or if you crave excitement, take a plunge in our pool with thrilling waterslides. Rejuvenate at our state-of-the-art SPA Cenvaree, or indulge in culinary delights at our selection of restaurants and bars. Choose from our modern accommodation options on Karon Beach - be it spacious family rooms, romantic suites, or gorgeous secluded villas, we offer the ideal retreat for every guest.

Whether you're looking for a family adventure or a peaceful escape, Centara Karon Resort Phuket caters to every whim, ensuring a memorable holiday for all ages.
