Deluxe Suite Private Pool

Wake up to sublime views of the Andaman Sea and soft lapping sounds of waves. An idyllic hideaway for endless family fun or romance, this 52-sqm Phuket hotel room with private pool, this Deluxe Suite Private Pool, features a furnished balcony and private plunge pool on site. Enjoy the Phuket sunset from your lounger or swim in the cool, private waters.

Good to Know

Check-In Time & Policies

Check-in is at 3:00 pm and check-out time is 11:00 am. Cancellations for a hotel room reservation must be received before 6:00 pm prior to the expected day of arrival.

Early Arrivals & Late Departures

Guests may request early check-in or late check-out upon making the reservation, and is subject to availability. Guests checking in or out before or after the designated periods may be charged an additional fee if not notified or requested in advance.

Children & Extra Beds

  • Complimentary stay for children less than 12 years of age sharing parents room and bedding (up to a maximum of 2 children, depending on room type)

  • Extra bed or sofa bed charges for adult or child may apply if requested and confirmed

  • Baby cot is complimentary and available upon request

Payment, Cancellation, and Non-Arrival Policies

  • All reservations must be guaranteed with a credit card number that is valid throughout the entire stay.

  • One night's charge is applied if the reservation is being cancelled less than 48 hours prior to arrival.

  • One night's charge is applied in the event of non-arrival, shortened stay or early departure.

  • Booking amendment is permitted, subject to space availability at the time of reservation.

Credit Cards

We accept Visa, Mastercard, JCB, Diners Club International and American Express credit cards.

For Families & Special Needs

Available on Request

Check-in is at 3:00 p.m., and check-out is at 11:00 a.m. Cancellations for hotel room reservations must be received 24 hours prior to the expected day of arrival for city properties and 48 hours for resort hotels.

Families can be accommodated in connecting rooms and/or suites – we recommend contacting the Hotel directly to make the best arrangements.

If you are seeking comfort, privacy and a truly memorable Phuket escape – this is your own little slice of paradise, soaked in golden light and a generous dose of indulgence. Tucked discreetly within Centara Grand Beach Resort Phuket, the Deluxe Suite Private Pool is made for those who appreciate the finer things in life. Endless sea views, private dips, and space to do absolutely nothing or everything, this elegant suite offers the perfect balance of relaxation and fun for couples and families.

At 52 square metres, this Phuket hotel room with private pool is more than just a place to sleep. It’s where slow mornings begin with the sound of waves drifting in from Karon Bay and end with a glass in hand on a sun lounger as the Andaman sky turns to gold. The private plunge pool is always the right temperature and ready for that impromptu dip.

Inside, the suite delivers on comfort with crisp bedding, a rainfall shower, a soaking tub, and thoughtful details that make you feel quietly spoiled. Outside, you’ve got a furnished balcony that calls for morning coffee rituals and sunset lounging sessions. It’s the kind of space where you lose track of time in the best way.

As part of a Phuket resort with private pool options, Centara Grand offers direct beach access, an exciting water park, and world-class dining - making it a complete holiday destination. It’s a rare find among hotels in Phuket, Thailand with private pool access that caters equally well to adventurous families and couples seeking seclusion. And if you do venture out, you’ll find the best of Phuket’s top attractions are within easy reach - then return to your private little world for a soak and a sigh of contentment.

Guests looking for a hotel room with private swimming pool in Phuket will appreciate the privacy and exclusivity this suite offers. Unlike a shared pool experience, your personal plunge pool is perfect for a peaceful dip at any time of day.

Whether you consider it your own private pool villa in Phuket or a luxury suite by the sea, the Deluxe Suite Private Pool promises a stay defined by calm mornings, sunny afternoons, and restful nights. Because let’s face it, once you’ve stayed at a Phuket hotel with a private pool like this, it’s hard to go back to anything else.

Ready to press pause on real life with Centara? Contact us to reserve the Deluxe Suite Private Pool and dive into your dream stay.
